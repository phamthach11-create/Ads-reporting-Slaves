# Google Ads Advisor prompts

Replace bracketed dates with the requested reporting periods. Keep the Pelican-only restriction.

## Performance analysis

> Give me a concise performance analysis for [current month] versus [previous month], including only campaigns with the word Pelican in the campaign name. Cover spend, impressions, clicks, CPC, CTR, leads, CPL, campaign-level drivers, and a short recommendation. Do not include any non-Pelican campaigns.

## Keyword analysis

> Give me a concise [current month] keyword analysis for campaigns whose name contains Pelican. Structure it as: 1) Top Pelican Keywords, 2) Search Intent & Competition, 3) Ad Engagement. Use the account data available to Ask Advisor. Keep the analysis brief enough for one presentation slide and do not include non-Pelican campaigns.

## Age and gender

> Give me a concise age and gender performance analysis for [current month], including only campaigns whose name contains Pelican. Use Google Ads demographic data, cover clicks and conversions by age and gender, identify the strongest segments, and provide one short recommendation. Keep it brief enough for one presentation slide and do not include non-Pelican campaigns.

## Devices

> Give me a concise device performance analysis for [current month], including only campaigns whose name contains Pelican. Use Google Ads device data, covering clicks, cost, conversions and efficiency across mobile phones, computers and tablets, with one recommendation. Keep it brief enough for one presentation slide and do not include non-Pelican campaigns.

## Ad schedule

> Give me a concise ad schedule performance analysis for [current month], including only campaigns whose name contains Pelican. Use day-of-week and hour-of-day data, identify the strongest and weakest times by clicks and conversions, and provide one recommendation. Keep it brief enough for one presentation slide and do not include non-Pelican campaigns.

## Advisor output checks

- Confirm the prompt requests the intended reporting dates and Pelican-only scope. Use the response directly; do not cross-check its metrics against tables or cards.
- Remove statements based on external web sources unless the user requests them.
- Replace vague superlatives with measured comparisons.
- Shorten prose without changing numbers or causal uncertainty.

## Matched locations

> Give me a concise matched-location analysis for [current month], only campaigns whose names contain Pelican, including enabled and paused campaigns with historical data. Use Matched locations at Regions level. Cover cost, conversions, grouped cost/conversion, non-converting spend and measured geographic opportunities. Use combined Pelican Region data available to Advisor, not all-account or a single campaign as the combined total.
